I miss my brother. He died suddenly a year ago today. It probably didn't feel sudden to him, and it wasn't much of a surprise to anyone else who had witnessed his struggles over what feels like the entirety of his adult life. I sought counseling to help with the loss but it was more to determine if I was, in fact, coping with it.
We determined that I had and was but I'm not so sure anymore. My memories of him are mostly stale. I saw him about a month before he died and we had a nice time. He seemed like he was getting to a good place. But as a person who has long-term sobriety and who struggled greatly with addiction, I was, of course skeptical nad measured in my enthusiasm about his recovery.
