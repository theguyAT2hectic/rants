 <img src="/img/soles_edit.png" style="float: left; margin-right: 15px; margin-bottom: 10px; max-width: 100px;"> This is everyone's favorite part of the bank job. Our guys - we're usually rooting for the robbers - are letting everyone know that they have no intention of committing wanton violence; they're here for the bank's money, not your money. You have no reason to be a hero, it will gain you nothing and we'll be forced to maintain control whatever means necessary. If you do not cooperate, people will get hurt, it will be your fault, and we'll all be back where we started but with more bumps, bruises, or holes in us.
The likelihood of experiencing this particular situation is low. I would love to watch a gang of tactically competent, smooth-ass criminals walk out of a bank with bags of money. What is more common is the tacit threat from an abusor, an oppressor, or an authority. "Behave." "Don't make me hurt you." "Don't force me to respond." "Do not make waves." "Do what you're told or there will be consequences." More palatably, "everything is fine, just sit still. We've got this covered."

There's a video of Sinead doing "The Emperor's New Clothes" from 1990 you can find on YouTube {{< youtube 3c0jxooNClM >}}. <img src="/img/sinead.png" style="float: right; margin-right: 15px; margin-bottom: 10px; max-width: 300px;"> This is from a show in Rotterdam and there is an accompanying performance of "Last Day of Our Acquaintance." The latter is one of the most moving and emotionally fraught things I've ever seen and it has moved me to little tears of amazement at times. The former is just a badass girl throwing down and having fun.
