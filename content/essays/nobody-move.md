This is everyone's favorite part of the bank job. Our guys - we're usually rooting for the robbers - are letting everyone know that they have no intention of committing wanton violence; they're here for the bank's money, not your money. You have no reason to be a hero, it will gain you nothing and we'll be forced to maintain control whatever means necessary. If you do not cooperate, people will get hurt, it will be your fault, and we'll all be back where we started but with more bumps, bruises, or holes in us.
The likelihood of experiencing this particular situation is low. I would love to watch a gang of tactically competent, smooth-ass criminals walk out of a bank with bags of money. What is much more likely is that it a the tacit warning from an adversary, an abuser,  or an oppressor.
"Behave." "Don't make me hurt you." "Don't force me to respond." "Do not make waves." "Do what you're told or there will be consequences." More palatably, "everything is fine, just sit still. We've got this covered."
